## 1. Who needs a commercial license

Use of the software is **free** — no agreement to sign, no registration, no
royalty, no headcount limit — if:

> your company had **less than 1,000,000 USD** total revenue in the prior tax
> year (2019 dollars, CPI-adjusted per [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)).

A commercial license is required as soon as your company's **total revenue in
the prior tax year reaches or exceeds that threshold**, whether or not any of
that revenue came from the software.

The trigger is **revenue**, not profit, not taxable income, and not headcount.
A company with 5,000 employees and $600,000 of revenue is under the threshold.
A company with 3 employees and $2,000,000 of revenue is over it.

**Educational use never needs a commercial license**, whatever the revenue:
teaching, learning, instruction at schools, universities and other educational
institutions, and academic or non-commercial research are permitted free under
[LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md#educational-use). A university with $2 billion of revenue
can teach a course with the software; it needs a commercial license only to
build the software into something it sells or provides to others, or to run its
own business systems on it.

## 3. Attributable Revenue

**Attributable Revenue** means the revenue your company recognizes, in the
relevant year, from products or services whose operation uses the software.

It **includes** revenue from a product that embeds, links, calls, or is served
by the software, including where the software runs on your infrastructure and
the customer never sees it.

It **excludes** revenue from products and services that do not use the software.

Where a single product only partly uses the software, Attributable Revenue is
that product's revenue. The licensor does not require apportionment within a
product, and will not audit for it.

So that cost is predictable before anyone commits engineering time to it. If
this engine ends up in production somewhere large, the licensing conversation
should be a signature, not a negotiation. The ceiling is stated up front and in
public precisely so nobody has to ask what happens at scale.
